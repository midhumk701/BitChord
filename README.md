> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Rumi Music is under development.** This is an early preview, not a stable release. Some screens still use the original BitChord design. The vinyl player and style switching are experimental and require device testing.

## About Rumi Music

Rumi Music is a customized, open-source Android music player with a charcoal-and-lavender visual direction. It retains the underlying BitChord playback experience while developing its own appearance and player styles.

## Features

- **Vinyl player (experimental):** Spinning record artwork with the current song's album cover at its center.
- **Classic player:** Keep the original BitChord Now Playing experience.
- **Switch styles (experimental):** Long-press the player artwork.
- **Music:** BitChord's existing playback, library, playlists, search, downloads, queue, and lyrics functionality.
- **Rumi branding:** Rumi Music app name and lavender design accents.

## Download

**Preview builds:** [GitHub Actions — Rumi preview APK](https://github.com/midhumk701/BitChord/actions/workflows/rumi-preview.yml)

Open a successful workflow run, scroll to **Artifacts**, download **Rumi-preview-APKs**, extract the ZIP, and install the Android APK. You may need to sign in to GitHub.

A normal **Releases** download will be added once the app is tested and ready for distribution.
